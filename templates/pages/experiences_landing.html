Experience Design Standards

What Makes an Experience "Signature"

A distinctive title, a meaningful purpose, an elegant visual identity, a thoughtful atmosphere and — whenever possible — time for reflection at the close. Every Signature Experience is designed to be remembered, not simply attended.
