Why Belong?

A Community Built on Shared Values

Creative Women Society is a place where women connect through extraordinary experiences, inspiring conversations and authentic relationships. Members become part of a community that celebrates culture, lifelong learning, elegance and mutual support.

Membership provides opportunities to discover new experiences, build lasting friendships and continue growing within a welcoming and inspiring environment.

Membership Benefits

A Growing Collection of Curated Opportunities

Experiences

Priority access to Signature Experiences, early booking for limited places, member rates, and invitations to exclusive Society Gatherings.

Community

A supportive community, opportunities to meet inspiring women, and meaningful professional and personal connections.

Learning

Leadership & Professional Growth sessions, Literary Afternoons & Book Club, cultural experiences, guest speakers and masterclasses.

Visibility

The opportunity to be featured in Member Spotlight, contribute to The Journal, lead workshops, and explore professional collaborations.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions

Who can become a member?

Women who value culture, lifelong learning, meaningful relationships and personal growth, from a wide range of professional backgrounds.

How do I apply?

Complete the Become a Member form below, and a member of the Society will be in touch to guide you through the next steps.

Is membership monthly or annual?

Both options are available — see Membership Options above.

Can I attend without membership?

Some Signature Experiences are open to guests; full membership provides priority access and member rates.

Can I bring a guest?

Guest policies vary by experience — details are shared ahead of each event.

How many experiences take place each year?

The Society offers a balanced calendar across Arts & Culture, Literary Experiences, Society Gatherings and Signature Journeys throughout the year.

How do I renew my membership?

Renewal details will be sent directly to members ahead of each membership period.
