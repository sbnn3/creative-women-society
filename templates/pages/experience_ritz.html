Society Gatherings

The Ritz Society Experience

An afternoon of elegance, conversation and connection.

Description

A quintessentially British afternoon, set within one of London's most celebrated addresses. Members gather for afternoon tea, conversation and the particular kind of elegance that comes from simply slowing down together — a signature expression of everything Creative Women Society stands for.

Why It Is Special

The Ritz Society Experience is more than afternoon tea — it is a celebration of British hospitality, elegance and the pleasure of unhurried conversation. It is designed as a signature moment in the Society's calendar, one that new members often remember as their first true introduction to the community.

What to Expect

A warm welcome, a beautifully set table, a curated selection of teas, sandwiches, scones and pastries, and the company of fellow members in an atmosphere of genuine conversation rather than networking. Time is left, quite deliberately, for nothing in particular.

Dress Code

Smart elegant. Many members choose to make an occasion of it — this is, after all, an afternoon about refinement and care in the details.

Date & Location

Held periodically throughout the year in central London. Exact dates, times and booking details are announced to members ahead of each sitting, with places offered on a priority basis to Society members.
