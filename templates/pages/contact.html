Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions

How can I become a member?

Visit the Membership page and complete the Become a Member form — a member of the Society will be in touch.

How often do experiences take place?

The Society offers a balanced calendar of Arts & Culture, Literary, Society Gatherings and Signature Journeys experiences throughout the year.

Can I attend an experience before becoming a member?

Some experiences are open to guests — please get in touch to ask about a specific event.

Can organisations collaborate with Creative Women Society?

Yes — the Society welcomes partnerships with cultural institutions, hospitality venues, publishers, charities and more. Use the form above with "Partnerships" as the subject.

How can I contribute to The Journal?

Members may submit articles reflecting the Society's values. Get in touch to discuss a contribution.
