About Us

Our Story

Creative Women Society was born from the remarkable community created through Creative English Language Academy.

Over time, many women continued to grow beyond the classroom. They developed businesses, advanced in their careers, discovered new passions and formed meaningful friendships. Creative Women Society was created to strengthen those relationships and offer a space where women continue to connect through extraordinary experiences, culture, lifelong learning and genuine human connection.

Today, the Society continues to welcome women who share these values and wish to become part of a community built on elegance, curiosity and belonging.

Vision

To become one of the most inspiring communities for women in the United Kingdom and beyond

Recognised for its elegant identity, meaningful experiences, cultural enrichment and lasting positive impact.

Mission

To bring remarkable women together through carefully curated experiences

Fostering meaningful connections, cultural enrichment, professional growth and lifelong learning within a supportive and inspiring community.

The Society Code

A Reflection of the Culture We Cultivate Together

Creative Women Society is built on shared values. Every member is encouraged to contribute to an atmosphere defined by respect, kindness, curiosity, professionalism, elegance in communication, openness to learning, mutual encouragement, authenticity, and confidentiality where appropriate.

The Society Code is not a list of restrictions, but a reflection of the culture we wish to cultivate together.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions

Who can join?

Creative Women Society welcomes women from diverse professional backgrounds who value lifelong learning, authentic relationships and elegant experiences.

Do I need to be an entrepreneur?

Not at all. The Society welcomes entrepreneurs, educators, healthcare professionals, lawyers, artists, consultants, managers and other accomplished professionals alike.

Can I attend without membership?

Some experiences are open to guests. Full details are available on the Membership page.

How often do experiences take place?

The Society offers a balanced calendar throughout the year, combining Arts & Culture, Literary Experiences, Society Gatherings and Signature Journeys.

Can I bring a guest?

Guest policies vary by experience. Please enquire ahead of a specific event.

How do I become a member?

Visit the Membership page to learn more and begin your application.
