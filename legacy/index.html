Annual Review

A Tradition, Year After Year

At the end of every year, Creative Women Society publishes an Annual Review celebrating the journey of the Society — experiences organised, cultural visits, books discussed, new members, charity initiatives, awards presented and highlights of the year, creating a historical archive of the Society's evolution.
